Create a complete, high-fidelity, responsive web platform called MetriCheck for an AI-assisted Legal Metrology Compliance System for packaged commodities.
The platform must look like a premium modern product, not a generic government portal, not a generic SaaS dashboard, and not a dark cyberpunk AI interface.
The entire experience should feel:
Professional + premium + modern + slightly futuristic + soft + visually rich + highly usable

The visual tone should sit exactly between two extremes:
- not too bright
- not too dark
Use a balanced semi-dark aesthetic with warm light content surfaces and deep muted teal navigation.
The interface should be visually beautiful, but functionality and clarity must always come first.
1. Product purpose
MetriCheck converts product-package images into a structured compliance assessment.
It supports:
Consumer
Scan a package and understand whether important declarations appear present, readable, and complete.
Producer
Pre-check packaging before printing or release and identify issues early.
Inspector / Compliance Team
Review evidence, findings, violations, history, and reports.
Admin
Manage platform access and records.
AI Compliance Assistant
Explain findings in simple language and suggest the next corrective step.
Important system principle:
AI extracts, classifies, and explains.
The rule engine decides PASS / WARNING / FAIL.

Do not imply that the AI assistant itself makes the legal compliance decision.
